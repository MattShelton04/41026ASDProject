# Planning, hazard and boundary layers

Property data registers ten reference datasets from NSW and ABS publishers: cadastral lots, five planning control layers, bushfire prone land, flood planning controls, 2021 ABS locality and LGA boundaries, gazetted suburb boundaries, school catchment zones, strata schemes, selected amenities and the ABS consumer price index.

## What they are for

Each dataset keeps the publisher's own features, attributes, edition, coordinate system and coverage, so other research areas can use them without reinterpreting the source. They are published as their own versions, like other datasets.

## What they cannot show

- A feature near or over a property is not a verified match to that property, and it is not a decision about which planning rule legally applies. Planning certificates and councils decide that.
- A missing or invalid shape does not show that a property is free of a planning control, bushfire risk or flooding.
- Flood planning controls cover only areas where councils have published controls. Many flood-prone areas are not mapped.
- A school catchment zone does not confirm enrolment eligibility.
- CPI is a Sydney and national price index. It is not a local property price index.

## Current availability

These datasets are registered but may not be loaded on this installation. Check Published data before relying on any of them. Interpreting these layers for a site belongs to the Site and planning research area.
