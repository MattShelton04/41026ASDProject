# ABS SEIFA 2021 area scores

The Area context tab on a property shows ABS SEIFA 2021 scores for the suburb or locality the property is in. SEIFA (Socio-Economic Indexes for Areas) ranks areas using 2021 Census data.

## The four indexes

- IRSD, relative socio-economic disadvantage. A low score means more disadvantage. A high score means less disadvantage, not necessarily advantage.
- IRSAD, relative socio-economic advantage and disadvantage. Low means more disadvantage, high means more advantage.
- IER, economic resources, such as income and housing costs.
- IEO, education and occupation.

## Reading a decile

Each index has a score and an Australia decile. Decile 1 is the lowest-scoring 10% of areas in Australia for that index; decile 10 is the highest-scoring 10%. The four indexes measure different things, so compare like with like. Where the ABS published no score for an area, PropertyScope shows it as unavailable, not zero.

## Limits

SEIFA describes an area, not a particular property, household or person. The boundaries are 2021 ABS suburbs and localities, which can differ from current gazetted suburbs. A property is matched to an area by exact locality name within NSW; if the match is ambiguous no score is shown.
