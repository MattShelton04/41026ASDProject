# Datasets in Property data

Property data registers 16 datasets. Registering a dataset means PropertyScope knows where to get it and how to check it. It does not mean the data is loaded. Whether a dataset has a published version on this installation is shown on the Published data and Data sources pages, and the assistant can check it with the data coverage and release tools.

## Foundational property data

- NSW address records (G-NAF Open NSW, Geoscape Australia, updated quarterly). Every property record in search comes from this dataset.
- NSW property sales (NSW Valuer General property sales information, PSI). Annual archives from 1990 plus weekly files for the current year.
- Example NSW property records. A three-record synthetic fixture used for tests and offline demonstrations. It is not real property data.

## Geography and boundaries

- ABS 2021 statistical localities (SAL) and local government areas (LGA).
- NSW gazetted suburb boundaries.
- NSW cadastral lots (NSW Spatial Services).
- NSW strata schemes.

## Planning and hazards

- NSW planning controls: five published environmental planning instrument layers, including zoning.
- NSW bushfire prone land (NSW Rural Fire Service).
- NSW published flood planning controls.

## Community and amenities

- NSW Bureau of Crime Statistics and Research (BOCSAR) recorded crime by postcode and suburb.
- NSW government school locations and school catchment zones.
- Selected official amenity layers.

## Economic context

- ABS SEIFA 2021 socio-economic indexes for NSW suburbs and localities.
- ABS consumer price index (CPI), all groups, Sydney and Australia.

## Which research area uses each dataset

Property data (Feature 1) owns acquisition, checking and publication for all 16. Sales history is also delivered to Sales and market (Feature 2), and crime and school locations to Suburb context (Feature 3). Those features import published versions into their own databases and own their own analysis.
