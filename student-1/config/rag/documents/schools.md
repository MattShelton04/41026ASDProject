# School locations and catchments

Property data holds two school datasets from the NSW Department of Education.

## School locations

Each record is one NSW government school with its school code, name, type, operational status (including closed schools), locality, local government area and coordinates. Suburb context imports this dataset to show nearby schools.

## School catchment zones

The catchment dataset holds the published primary, secondary and future intake zones as shapes.

## Limits

A school's location says nothing about its quality, capacity or whether it has places. Being inside a catchment zone is not confirmation of eligibility; the school and the Department of Education decide enrolment. Distances and nearby-school lists are calculated by the Suburb context research area, not by Property data.
