FEATURE 02Planned

Sales & market intelligence

Recorded sale history, deterministic comparable evidence, suburb trends and market-case CRUD—carefully separated from valuation or forecasting.

  • Sale timeline and exclusions
  • Comparable method and sample size
  • Evidence-bounded AI explanation
FEATURE 03Planned

Suburb, crime & liveability context

Saved suburb comparisons, schools and practical context, plus neutral crime-series analysis with explicit count/rate and zero/missing semantics.

  • Same-period comparisons
  • Source and denominator notes
  • No safety or desirability ranking
FEATURE 04Planned

Site, planning & building due diligence

Source-aware constraints, coverage, strata/building observations and a site-review workflow that drafts questions for qualified verification rather than certifying safety or compliance.

  • Intersection versus unavailable coverage
  • Layer provenance and effective date
  • Review checklist and AI question pack
FEATURE 05Planned

Buyer workspace & dossier

Profiles, watchlists, shortlist comparisons, follow-up tasks and human-reviewed dossiers that compose partial evidence from Features 1–4.

  • Reviewable report lifecycle
  • Stakeholder questions and tasks
  • Printable source-linked dossier

Decision support, not professional advice.

PropertyScope is an independent student research tool. It is not affiliated with the NSW Government and does not provide valuation, conveyancing, planning, engineering, insurance, lending, enrolment or buy/no-buy advice. Source coverage and uncertainty must be verified for material decisions.
