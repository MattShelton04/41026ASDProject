# What Site and due diligence does

Site and due diligence is a pre-purchase research workspace for one property at a time. You record
a site review against a verified property, gather the planning, environmental, strata and building
evidence held for it, work through a due-diligence checklist, and turn the gaps into clear
questions a buyer can put to a suitably qualified professional.

It is research support. It does not certify that a property complies with any control, confirm it
is safe or structurally sound, decide whether it is legally suitable, or replace advice from a
licensed professional.

## Where the evidence comes from

The planning, environmental, strata and building records come from this feature's own database,
seeded for the project rather than fetched live from any council or authority. The property itself
is confirmed against the shared Property data area before a review is saved; when that area is
unavailable the property shows as unavailable rather than blocking your work.

Each evidence record carries its record type, an evidence state, the source it came from and a
confidence. Flood and bushfire exposure is shown as map layers checked against the property
location. Nothing is retrieved from the internet when you open a review.

Current record counts, review contents and specific evidence values are live database facts. Ask
the assistant for those; they are answered from the feature's read-only tools, not from this
guidance.

## How to record a site review

Choose New review, select the property by its address, confirm it is the one you meant, and give
the review a title. Set its status as you progress, for example draft while you are still gathering
evidence.

Work through the checklist for the planning, environmental, strata and building matters that apply,
and add notes that explain what you found or still need to confirm. When you are ready, ask the
assistant to generate professional-verification questions; it grounds them in the evidence states
and prioritises whatever is unavailable or only partially covered.
