# What Site and due diligence cannot establish

The evidence here is a record of what was gathered for a property, not a determination about it.
Recording that a control, hazard or building matter was checked is not the same as certifying the
property against it. A site review is not a building inspection, a planning certificate, a survey
or legal advice, however complete it looks.

## Questions this feature cannot answer

Site and due diligence cannot tell you whether a property complies with a planning control or the
Building Code, whether it is safe or structurally sound, whether a past change was approved, or
whether it is a suitable purchase. It cannot value the property, assess contract terms, or say
whether a price is fair.

Those determinations belong to the relevant authority or a licensed professional: a certifier or
council for compliance, a building inspector or engineer for condition, a surveyor for boundaries,
and a conveyancer or lawyer for title and contract. Questions about recorded sale prices or market
value belong to the Sales and market research area, not here.

## Coverage gaps and unavailable evidence

A review reports the evidence it holds and, separately, where evidence is unavailable or only
partially covers the property. Unavailable evidence is a gap to confirm with the source or a
professional. It is not proof that a constraint is absent or that a matter is settled.

A mapped hazard layer that does not intersect the property lowers concern but does not remove the
need to confirm, because the layer may be indicative or out of date. Where the evidence genuinely
cannot support an answer, the assistant should say the context is insufficient rather than guess.

## Demonstration data

Some reviews include deterministic synthetic showcase evidence so the workflow can be demonstrated
without real records. Where that is so, treat those figures as an illustration of the mechanics
only. They must never be quoted as evidence about a real property, and no conclusion about an
actual site, building or approval should be drawn from them.
