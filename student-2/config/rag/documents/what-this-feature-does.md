# What Sales and market does

Sales and market is a property sales research workspace. You pick a verified property, review the
sale records attributed to it, narrow them with a date window and a match-quality threshold, and
save that work as a market case you can return to and edit.

It is research support. It does not produce a valuation, forecast a price, recommend whether to
buy, or replace professional advice.

## Where the sale records come from

Sale observations arrive as a checksum-protected release published by the Property data research
area, which sources them from NSW property sales information. Sales and market does not collect
records from publishers directly and does not fetch anything from the internet when you open a
case.

Each sale carries its contract date, recorded price, match tier, match confidence, the source
release it arrived in, and whether the record is synthetic demonstration data. Every summary names
the source releases it drew on, so a number on screen can be traced back to the release that
supplied it.

Current record counts, release identifiers and case contents are live database facts. Ask the
assistant for those; they are answered from the feature's read-only tools rather than from this
guidance.

## How to build a market case

Choose New case, give it a descriptive name, and select the property by its address. Confirm the
address is the one you meant, then set the start and end dates for the period you want to study.

Set the status to Draft, Active, Complete or Archived, and choose the minimum match tier. Tier A
is the strictest; the default minimum is tier B. Add research notes if they help you remember why
the case exists, then choose Save case.

Selecting a saved case from the Market cases list shows its eligible sale count, median recorded
price, excluded-record count, source-release count, and volume by contract year. Use Edit to
change the name, date window, status, match threshold or notes. Edits use optimistic version
checking, so if the case changed elsewhere since you opened it, your update is rejected rather
than silently overwriting the other change.
