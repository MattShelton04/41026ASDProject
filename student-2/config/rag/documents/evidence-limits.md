# What Sales and market cannot establish

The evidence here is a record of what sales were registered, not an assessment of what a property
is worth. Recorded prices are historical transactions. They do not establish current market value,
and a summary over them is not a valuation however many records it covers.

## Questions this feature cannot answer

Sales and market cannot tell you what a property is worth today, what it will be worth later,
whether a listed price is fair, or whether you should buy. It holds no listing prices, no rental
data, no agent appraisals and no auction results, so it cannot compare a sale against what a
property was advertised for.

It cannot explain why a particular sale reached its price. Condition, renovations, inclusions,
relationship between the parties and terms of sale are not in the source records, so two sales of
similar properties may differ for reasons this feature cannot see.

Questions about crime, schools, planning controls, flood or bushfire exposure belong to other
research areas. Questions about value, structural condition or contract terms belong to a licensed
valuer, building inspector or conveyancer.

## Coverage gaps and excluded records

A summary reports only the sales that passed the case filters, and separately counts what it left
out. Records are excluded for four reasons: no contract date, a contract date outside the case
period, a missing or zero price, or a match tier below the threshold you selected.

Excluded records are not errors and are not deleted. Raising the minimum match tier deliberately
narrows the evidence, so a case with few eligible sales may reflect a strict threshold rather than
a quiet market.

Where fewer than three sales are eligible, the summary states that this is insufficient evidence.
Treat the median from such a case as an observation about those specific records, not a
representative figure.

## Demonstration data

Some cases include deterministic synthetic showcase data so the workflow can be demonstrated
without real records. Where that is so, the summary says the case includes synthetic showcase
data. Those figures exist to show the mechanics and must never be quoted as evidence about a real
property or a real market.

A case whose records are synthetic cannot support any claim about actual sales activity, and no
conclusion about a real suburb, street or property should be drawn from one.
