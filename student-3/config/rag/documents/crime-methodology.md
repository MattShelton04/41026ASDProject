# How to interpret recorded crime evidence

## Counts and rates

A count is the number of recorded observations in the selected source categories and period. A
rate uses a stated population denominator and is not interchangeable with a count. Feature 3 must
not mix measures within one comparison. Where a recent count is divided by older population
context, the different reference periods must be disclosed.

## Zero and missing

A recorded zero is meaningful only inside the source's declared observation universe. Missing or
unavailable evidence remains null and must not be plotted or described as zero. Sparse coverage
does not support an assumption about unobserved months.

## Responsible interpretation

Recorded crime data does not establish why events occurred, an individual's risk, the safety of a
place or future crime. A comparison is descriptive evidence for a controlled period and category,
not a suburb ranking.
