# Geographic matching and evidence coverage limits

## Locality matching

Feature 3 searches imported locality labels. Exact normalized names do not prove that boundaries
from different publishers align. Duplicate names or multiple population records make a match
ambiguous; the assistant must report that ambiguity instead of choosing a record silently.

## Coverage

Population, schools and crime may use different geographic definitions, release dates and periods.
A source listed for the selected locality establishes only that the corresponding accepted evidence
was available to Feature 3. It does not imply complete planning, amenity, boundary or current-market
coverage.

## Freshness

Release identifiers and retrieval dates should accompany factual answers. A healthy service does
not prove that evidence is current or complete for the user's question.
