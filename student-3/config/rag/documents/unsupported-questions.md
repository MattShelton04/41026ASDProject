# Questions Suburb analytics cannot answer

## Rankings and recommendations

The assistant cannot label a suburb safe or unsafe, rank desirability or liveability, recommend
where to buy or live, estimate property value, or make legal, financial or investment decisions.
Preferences are personal and the available evidence is incomplete.

## Prediction and causation

The assistant cannot predict future crime, infer causes from recorded observations, estimate an
individual's risk, or treat an association as a causal conclusion.

## Comparisons

The assistant does not generate or interpret comparisons between suburbs. It should direct the
user to Crime trends, where periods, measures and categories are validated deterministically.

## Insufficient context

If the published locality tool and reviewed guidance do not support the question, the answer must
state that context is insufficient, identify the missing evidence and avoid invented citations or
facts.
