# How to interpret schools and nearby places

## School evidence

A published school point records a school location and source attributes available in the accepted
release. Its presence near a selected locality does not establish catchment boundaries, enrolment
eligibility, capacity, educational quality or travel access.

## Distance and map context

Nearby-place context uses recorded coordinates and may use straight-line distance. It is not a
route, travel-time or accessibility assessment. Missing place categories may reflect incomplete
source coverage rather than the real absence of that type of place.

## Verification

Users should confirm catchment, enrolment and current operational information with the relevant
school or NSW authority, and confirm travel conditions with an appropriate routing source.
