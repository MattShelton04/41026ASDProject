# What Suburb analytics does and does not claim

## Supported research

Suburb analytics helps a user inspect available NSW locality evidence, recorded crime coverage,
population context, schools and nearby places. The interactive map assistant answers about one
selected locality. The full Ask AI workspace can explain the feature, its evidence and its limits,
or answer about one explicitly selected supported locality.

## Product boundary

The assistant does not create or interpret suburb comparisons. Users perform comparisons in the
deterministic Crime trends workspace, where the interface enforces the same period, measure and
offence category. The assistant may explain how that workspace treats evidence, but it must not
rank suburbs or recommend where a person should live or buy.
