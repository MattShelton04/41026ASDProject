# Limits of buyer-workspace evidence

## Guidance

Bounds: 10 properties, 10 target suburbs, 10 locality requests, 25 saved Sales research/Due diligence records. Non-empty is not complete; retain partial coverage, excluded sales and synthetic-data limits.

Suburb analytics reads published context using exact uppercase/whitespace-normalised NSW names. Targets are buyer preferences; property locations require verified Property discovery identities. Do not substitute postcodes or address labels. Names do not prove boundary equivalence. Missing records mean unavailable, not zero crime; never substitute demo data. Duplicate population matches need verification.

Retain provenance, periods and limits; cap suburb categories at 25 records/64 KiB and disclose omissions. Population is 2021 context, not a current crime-rate denominator. Schools imply no catchment eligibility. Do not rank safety or infer causation. Verify conflicting sales with primary sources; review flagged Due diligence with a qualified adviser. Coverage is not a valuation, legal advice or purchase recommendation.
