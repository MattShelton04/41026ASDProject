# Shortlists and journey stages

## Guidance

Add a property using its Property discovery reference. The workspace checks that reference through the public property API. Pending or unavailable validation does not establish a property identity. Review the address and source evidence before relying on it. Journey stages are Shortlisted, Inspecting, Reviewing, Offer Considered and Closed. They organise the buyer's workflow; changing a stage does not book an inspection, submit an offer or purchase a property. Ratings from one to five and low, medium or high priorities are personal assessments, not valuations.
